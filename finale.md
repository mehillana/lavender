# lavender. v13finale

### The final version.

Lavender is finished. **v13finale is the last version**: no more features, no more updates. It stays exactly as it is, free, local and yours.

---

## What "finale" means

- **Feature freeze.** Nothing new will be added. The feature set you have in v13finale is the feature set Lavender keeps.
- **One engine.** Mangosoft runs on **yakisoba-1**, the only engine. No model picker, no tiers, and the engine is frozen.
- **Design only.** The last work on Lavender was design: the final Yakisoba theme, the Mangosoft bar, the landing page and the small details (focus rings, selectors, scrollbars, motion).
- **No roadmap.** There are no planned versions. The README and the changelog no longer list future plans.

## What is inside

| | |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Day, Week, Month, Year and Agenda views, four kinds of time, a calendar per person, colours, starred filter, 12/24-hour time, undo and redo. |
| **Mangosoft bar** | Not a chat: a bar of selectors with three modes. **Create** (type, name, day, days, time, repeat, person, colour), **Change** (filter, then change many events at once) and **Ask** (summaries, free slots, conflicts, birthdays). Live preview, saved bars and `Ctrl+K`. |
| **Focus, Insights, Countdown** | Pomodoro with calm noise, weekly stats and a heatmap, days left until what matters. |
| **Habits and Quests** | Mood, water and habits at a glance, and eight tiny missions that grow your level. |
| **Themes and type** | Yakisoba (the default) and Ginger, plus the older families. Choose **Lavender Simple Google** (Google Sans) or **Lavender Github Cubed** (Mona Sans). |
| **Languages** | English, Català, Français, Aranés and 中文. |
| **Privacy** | No account, no servers, no tracking, no ads. Everything lives in your browser. Import and export with `.ics` and `.json`. |

## Keep it safe

Lavender keeps your data in your browser, and browsers can be dramatic about clearing things. Download a `.json` backup from Settings now and then. Because Lavender is finished, a backup you make today will keep working with it.
