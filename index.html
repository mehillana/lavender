01

Say it. It's scheduled.

The Smart bar reads plain English: dates, times, ranges, people and types. "Dinner with Marta Friday 8pm" becomes a real event before you lift a finger.

02

Notes live inside events.

Every event opens into a document made of blocks: headings, text, checklists and reminders. Take meeting notes, plan a trip, keep a memory.

03

One calendar per person.

Give your partner, your team, your kids and your future self their own colour. Show, hide and overlay them at a glance, with a legend that explains it all.

04

Quests build the habit.

Eight tiny missions teach every feature and grow your level from Seedling to Full bloom. Planning, finally, with momentum.

05

Seasons, holidays, moon.

Equinoxes, solstices, Easter, festive days and moon phases glow quietly in your calendar. Toggle them any time.

06

Built for speed.

Search, agenda view, repeating events, reminders, keyboard shortcuts (D, W, M, Y, T, C) and five soft themes from Lavender to Night.
